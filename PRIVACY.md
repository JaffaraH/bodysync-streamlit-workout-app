# BodySync privacy policy

BodySync is a free, personal fitness tracker. This page says what it does with your information.

**What we store.** Your workouts, lifts, playlists, skills, weekly plan, settings, the meals you save, and any exercises you create (including their photos).

**Where it is stored.** In your own Google account. Only you can see it, by signing in to BodySync. The people who run BodySync have no database and can't see your workouts, and there's no link anyone could find. (Technically it's Google Drive's private "app data" area: it doesn't appear among your Drive files and can't be shared.) To delete everything: Google Drive settings > Manage apps > BodySync > Delete hidden app data.

**On your own computer** (no sign-in), everything stays in a file on that computer.

**What we get from Google sign-in.** Your name and email address, used only to show "Signed in as ..." and to tell accounts apart inside the app. We do not store them anywhere else.

**What we do not do.** No ads, no selling or sharing of data, no analytics trackers.

**AI features (optional).** When you use FitBuddy or a meal idea, what you type (and a photo, if you add one) is sent to Google's Gemini service to get an answer. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use it to improve its products, so don't include anything private. If you paste your own Gemini key, it stays in your browser session and is not saved.

**Optional services.** If fun GIFs are on, your browser loads GIF images from GIPHY. Recipe search, if the app owner turned it on, sends your search text to Edamam.

**Contact.** Open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
